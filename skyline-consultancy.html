How It Works

From Dream to Degree
in 6 Simple Steps

1

Free Initial Consultation

Book a no-obligation session with our senior counselor to discuss your academic background, goals, budget, and preferred destinations.

2

Profile Evaluation & University Shortlisting

We analyze your profile and curate a personalized list of 8–12 universities across ambition, match, and safety categories.

3

Test Preparation & Documentation

IELTS/TOEFL coaching, SOP drafting, essay writing, and collation of all supporting documents required for applications.

4

Application Submission

We submit flawless applications on your behalf, track deadlines, and follow up with admissions offices to ensure your file stands out.

5

Offer Letter & Visa Filing

Once offers arrive, we guide you through choosing the best one, prepare your visa file, and coach you for your interview.

Travel briefing, accommodation arrangement, bank account guidance, SIM card setup — we make sure you land confident and prepared.
